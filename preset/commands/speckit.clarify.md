## speckit-pro rules

- Override upstream's total question cap: ask in rounds of up to five, one question
  at a time, then rescan until every material point is resolved or explicitly deferred.
- Material means behavior, acceptance, security, scope or verification changes.
  Do not repeat questions already settled by memory or the spec.
- Write accepted answers into the spec immediately. Record deferred points and what
  they block; propose project-wide questions through baseline.amend.
